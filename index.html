About this dashboard

A reporting & analytics deliverable built to demonstrate the work of a Senior Reporting Analyst in ACC's Health Analytics team. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, Tailwind & Recharts. All figures are calibrated to publicly-reported ACC statistics and shown for demonstration purposes only.
